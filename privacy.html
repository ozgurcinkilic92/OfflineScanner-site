Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Offline Scanner is a document scanner that works entirely on your device. The short version: the app collects no data, sends nothing to anyone, and has no servers.

Data we collect

We collect no data. The developer, Özgür Çınkılıç, does not receive your scans, documents, recognized text, photos, library contents, settings, usage statistics, crash reports or identifiers. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Where your data is processed and stored

All processing happens on your iPhone: scanning, cleanup, text recognition (Apple's on-device Vision framework), editing and export. Your documents are stored in the app's private storage inside your device. Other apps cannot read that storage.

No network transmission

The app never makes a network connection. It does not upload, sync or transmit anything. It works without internet access, and it has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign in to.

Sharing and saving are your choice

When you export a file, save it to Files, print it, share it or create a backup, the file goes only where you send it. Creating a backup produces a single zip file that you save wherever you choose. The app does not send that file anywhere itself.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan documents.
  • Photo Library: used only to import the photos you choose to scan.

The app does not request any other permission. Files you import through the Files app are read only for the import you start.

Purchases

Offline Scanner Pro is a one-time purchase made through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and manages your purchase under Apple's own privacy policy. The app receives no personal information from Apple beyond the confirmation that a purchase exists on your Apple Account, which it uses only to unlock Pro features on your device. Restoring a purchase uses the same Apple Account.

Third parties

The app uses no third-party services, SDKs, analytics, advertising networks or social media connections.

Children

Offline Scanner is not directed to children, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 or the equivalent age in your country.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Because the app collects no data, changes are unlikely to affect you, but we will tell you here if they do.
