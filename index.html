Offline Scanner Support

Offline Scanner turns paper documents into searchable PDFs, editable text and common file formats. Scanning, text recognition and all processing happen on your iPhone. The app never connects to the internet.

What the app does

  • Scan documents with the camera, or import photos and PDFs from Photos and Files.
  • Recognize text in English, Turkish and Spanish, and correct it on the page.
  • Export to PDF, searchable PDF, plain text, JPG and PNG, and save to Files or share.
  • Offline Scanner Pro (one-time purchase) adds text editing, Word (DOCX) and Markdown export, PDF tools and batch export.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. The app never makes a network connection. It works in airplane mode and with Wi-Fi and mobile data turned off. It has no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

Where are my documents stored?

Your documents stay in the app's private storage on your iPhone. Other apps cannot read them. A document leaves the device only when you export it, share it or save a backup, and only to the place you choose.

How do I back up my library and restore it?

Open Settings and tap Create Backup. The app makes one zip file, and you choose where to save it, for example in Files or on iCloud Drive. To restore, open Settings, tap Restore Backup and pick the zip file. Backups are made only when you ask for one. Keep the zip file safe, because it contains your documents.

How do I buy Offline Scanner Pro and restore it?

Pro is a one-time purchase made through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment, and the app receives no personal data. There is no subscription. To restore a purchase on a new device or after a reinstall, open Settings, tap Offline Scanner Pro and then Restore Purchases. Restoring uses the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Does it support Turkish and Spanish?

Yes. Text recognition works in English, Turkish and Spanish. With Recognition Language set to Automatic, the app detects which of the three languages a page uses. You can choose one language in Settings if the detection is wrong for your documents.

Can it convert handwriting?

Handwritten notes are transcribed for review. With Offline Scanner Pro you can convert a handwritten page to typed text and check the result. The app does not redraw handwriting on the page.

Why is text missing on some very dense pages on iOS 27?

On iOS 27, Apple's text recognition can return fewer lines than on iOS 26 for very dense pages. The app works around part of this, but a very dense page may still come back incomplete. Check the recognized text against the scan and correct it, or scan the page in smaller parts with good, even light.

Why does the app ask for camera and photo access?

The camera is used only to scan documents. Photo library access is used only for the photos you pick to import. Nothing is sent anywhere.
